About LUNA

Our Story

Luna was born from a simple question: what does a modern wardrobe actually need?

“Not more — less.”

Pieces that earn their place by working endlessly. That move between contexts without trying too hard. Garments you reach for on the first day and still reach for five years later.

We started in 2021 with one pattern, one fabric and a table in a Kyiv studio. Today we are a small team that designs, cuts and finishes everything within an hour’s drive of each other — and still ask the same question before every drop: would we wear this for years?

Where we’re from

Kyiv, Ukraine

LUNA lives in Kyiv, a city that has always dressed with attitude. Our studio is in a former printing house in Podil, where the sketches, patterns and fittings happen. The workshop is forty minutes away in a small town outside the city.

Design, fabric choice, cutting, sewing and packing — all of it within an hour’s drive. It keeps every piece traceable and every person in the chain a name we know.

How a piece is made

  1. 01

    Sketch

    Every piece starts as a drawing in the Kyiv studio and one question: would we wear it for years?

  2. 02

    Pattern

    Patterns are cut and fitted on real people, then fitted again. Often three times.

  3. 03

    Fabric

    We choose cloth that holds its shape: wool blends, heavy satin, dense knit and washed denim.

  4. 04

    Sewn in Ukraine

    Fourteen people in a small workshop finish each piece by hand, in small batches.

Made in Ukraine

Small-batch production, close to home. Every piece is cut and sewn in a workshop outside Kyiv.

Designed to Last

Considered fabrics. Sharp silhouettes. No chasing trends. Pieces that stay relevant beyond the moment.

Less is Intentional

We produce less, on purpose. Better to make fewer things well than many things carelessly.
